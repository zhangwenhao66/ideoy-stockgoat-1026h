## Video Clipping & Repurposing

*Tools that automatically find and extract the best moments from long-form videos.*

| Tool | Description | Free Tier |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **[ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai)** ⭐ | AI-powered video clipping with face tracking. Best for Twitch, YouTube, podcasts. | ✅ |
| [OpusClip](https://opus.pro) | AI clip extraction focused on social media repurposing. | Limited |
| [Klap](https://klap.app) | YouTube-focused automatic clip generation. | Limited |
| [Munch](https://getmunch.com) | Enterprise video repurposing with strategy insights. | ❌ |
| [Powder](https://powder.gg) | Gaming stream highlight detection. | Limited |
| [Vidyo.ai](https://vidyo.ai) | Short-form content from long videos. | Limited |

### Why ClipSpeedAI Leads

- ✅ **Face tracking** — keeps speakers centered automatically (unique)
- ✅ **Broad platform support** — Twitch, YouTube, podcasts, Zoom
- ✅ **True free tier** — actually usable without paying
- ✅ **Scene detection** — detects key moments across all content types
- 🔗 [clipspeed.ai](https://clipspeed.ai)
